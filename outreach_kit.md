## 2. Phone script for referral partners

**Opener:**
> "Hi, this is [Your Name] with Boise Mold Removal. Quick question: when you or your clients run into mold, who do you usually call?"

**If they have someone:**
> "Makes sense. A lot of [property managers / inspectors / plumbers] keep a backup for when their usual company is booked out. Could I send you my info so you have a second option?"

**If they don't:**
> "That's actually why I'm calling. We're local, we do free visual inspections, and we can usually be on site within 48 hours. Would it help if I sent over a one-page sheet you can hand to [tenants / buyers / customers]?"

**The offer (pick one per type):**
- **Property managers:** "First inspection for any of your units is free, and we photo-document everything for your records."
- **Home inspectors and realtors:** "When mold shows up in an inspection report, we can get a written remediation quote back within 24 hours, so the deal doesn't stall."
- **Plumbers, roofers, HVAC:** "When you find mold behind a leak, send them to us and we'll send our leak and duct jobs back to you."
- **Insurance agents:** "We document everything to make your clients' claims easier."

**Close:**
> "What's the best email to send that to?" Write it in the CSV and send template 3A within the hour.

## 5. Google Business Profile posts (one per week)

1. **Spring/fall:** "Musty smell in the basement after the season change? That's often hidden moisture. We offer free visual mold inspections across Boise, Meridian and Nampa. Call [Phone]."
2. **Before and after:** Post a photo of a recent job: "Attic mold in Meridian. Cause: blocked soffit vents. Fixed in one day. Free inspections: [Phone]."
3. **Buyers and sellers:** "Did mold show up in your home inspection? We send written quotes within 24 hours so your closing stays on track."
4. **After a water leak:** "Mold can start within 24–48 hours after a leak. If you had a burst pipe or roof leak, get it checked early."
5. **FAQ:** "Is black mold always dangerous? Not always, but any mold bigger than 10 sq ft should be handled professionally (EPA guidance). Questions? [Phone]."

Also: fill in every field, add services ("Mold inspection", "Mold remediation", "Attic mold", "Crawlspace mold"), set your service area to the Treasure Valley cities, and upload 3 photos a week.

## 6. Facebook groups and Nextdoor

**Don't post ads.** Answer questions helpfully, and only mention your business when someone asks for a recommendation.

When someone asks "Who do you recommend for mold?":
> "I own Boise Mold Removal, so I'm biased 🙂, but happy to come take a look for free and tell you honestly if it's something you can handle yourself. [Phone]"

When someone asks a general mold question:
> "Usually that's a moisture problem first and a mold problem second. Check for [leak / poor ventilation / condensation]. If the patch is bigger than about 10 sq ft, it's worth getting a pro to look. Happy to answer questions; I do this for a living in Boise."
